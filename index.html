We are John McClean and the Clan

Based out of London, John McClean and the Clan are known for their blistering live sets, featuring John's gritty and passionate vocals, original songs and the Clan's tight and funky backing. The band combines elements of the Blues, Soul, Gospel and Rock into their own unique and mighty sound.

Members

  • Vocals, Guitar – John McClean
  • Vocals, Lead Guitar – Alastair Murray
  • Vocals and Percussion – Jennie McClean
  • Bass – Gary Cain
  • Drums – Ben McClean
